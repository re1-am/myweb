About Us

Meet the Educator Behind Rexford Educates

I am Rexford Opare Amoako, a passionate Mathematics and ICT teacher with over a decade of experience shaping young minds across Ghana. This platform is my way of extending the classroom beyond four walls.

Here you'll find educative content, the latest GES updates, editable lesson notes, and tools that make teaching and learning easier, more effective, and more enjoyable.

I am also a Data Analyst and a Full Stack Web Developer (Python) who builds modern, functional web applications — and this website is living proof of that.
